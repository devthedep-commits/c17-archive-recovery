About the Project

Between 1998 and 2003, a large number of paper and electronic records from the former St. Maren Hospital were transferred to long-term storage. Portions of the original index were found to be incomplete or damaged.

The Archive Recovery Initiative was established to identify surviving records and restore associations where possible. Some recovered files remain unassigned because their original index information can no longer be verified.

A limited automated comparison service remains online for individuals wishing to check whether an unassigned archival reference may be associated with their contact information.
